3. CORE ECOSYSTEMS & PROGRAM PARTICIPATION
================================================================================
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. C1S C0DE WARR10RS (Warriors Portal Program)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Slogan: 
  "CODE BETTER. THINK SMARTER. WIN TOGETHER."
  "Stop brute-forcing your code from O(N²) to O(N log N). Learn how to optimize your logic and think like a grandmaster."

* Overview: 
  A peer-learning ecosystem focused on building consistency, mastering DSA, and cracking technical placements. Upgrade your logic, optimize your runtime.

* Core Features:
  1. Daily POTD (Problem of the Day): Handpicked LeetCode problems to build daily consistency.
  2. Weekly Bug Bounties: Spot the flaw, claim the bragging rights. Master debugging.
  3. Monthly Sprints: Virtual mock contests to sharpen your speed and problem-solving.
  4. Master DSA: From Arrays to Dynamic Programming, break down complex concepts with peers.
  5. Build Your Profile: Get step-by-step guidance on building a strong LeetCode profile and contest ratings.
  6. Competitive Edge: Helps you prepare for IEEE Xtreme, ICPC, and top technical placements.

* Registration Fields Required:
  - Name, UID, Email, Mobile, Department, Year (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, Postgrad), LeetCode Username or Profile URL, Password (min 6 characters), LinkedIn Profile (Optional), Referred By Team Member (Optional).

* Mentor Application Fields:
  - Name, Role (Student / Working Professional), Email, Mobile, Company/Role or Department, Year, LeetCode Username, Resume Link, Previous Mentoring Experience Description, LinkedIn Profile (Optional).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B. CIS INNOVATORS HUB
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Slogan:
  "BUILD. INNOVATE. RESEARCH."
  "10 Teams. 10 Projects. One Vision -- Innovation."

* Overview:
  A long-term innovation ecosystem where students build impactful projects, collaborate in teams, and represent IEEE CIS in national and international hackathons.

* The Journey:
  1. Ideathon Screening: Submit your problem statement and proposed solution. Best ideas get shortlisted.
  2. Team Formation: Got an idea but no team? We'll help you find like-minded collaborators.
  3. Structured Mentorship: Get technical guidance from seniors, faculty, and IEEE mentors.
  4. Hackathon Exposure: Represent IEEE CIS in Smart India Hackathon and international competitions.

* Participation Types:
  - Option A: Team (Max 5 members). Required Fields: Team Name, Other Team Members Names & UIDs, Project Idea & Tech Stack description.
  - Option B: Individual Match (Matched with teammates). Required Fields: Domain/Skills details of what kind of teammates you want.
  - Option C: Individual Later (Form team later before a given deadline, else auto-matched).

* Basic Fields Required (All Types):
  - Name (Leader / Individual), UID, Email, Mobile, Department, Year, Preferred Technical Domain.

5. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
================================================================================
Q1: How can I join IEEE CIS CUSB?
A1: You can join by participating in our annual membership drive or by reaching 
    out to any of our core team members during our events. Keep an eye on our 
    social media for official registration links.

Q2: Do I need to be a computer science student to join?
A2: Not at all! IEEE CIS welcomes students from all backgrounds who have an 
    interest in computational intelligence, AI, and related fields.

Q3: Are the events only for IEEE members?
A3: While some exclusive workshops are members-only, most of our flagship 
    events, hackathons, and expert talks are open to all students.

Q4: How can I stay updated about upcoming events?
A4: Follow us on our Instagram and LinkedIn pages, and regularly check the 
    Events section of this website!

6. PRIVACY POLICY & DATA STORAGE STATEMENTS
================================================================================
* Introduction:
  At IEEE Computational Intelligence Society CUSB, we are committed to protecting 
  your personal information and your right to privacy. Last updated: June 2026.

* Key Policy Sections:
  1. Information We Collect:
     - Personal Info: Name & Email when you sign in via Google.
     - Platform Data: External coding profiles (LeetCode) & submitted solutions 
       for the Code Warriors Portal.
     - Usage Data: Streak, leaderboard rankings, and event participation.
  2. How We Use Your Information:
     - Provide and maintain the Warriors Portal (streaks, global leaderboard).
     - Verify identity & manage secure access.
     - Communicate regarding events, hackathons, and community updates.
     - Improve platform and maintain safety.
  3. Data Security & Storage:
     - Secure storage with industry-standard encryption (via Supabase backend).
     - We DO NOT store passwords (authentication is via Google OAuth).
     - Strict access controls to guarantee confidentiality.
  4. Third-Party Sharing:
     - We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
     - Data shared ONLY with trusted infrastructure partners (Google Cloud, Supabase)
       solely to run the site and features.
